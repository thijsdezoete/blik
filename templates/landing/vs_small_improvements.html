Focused Feedback or Continuous Performance Management

Both tools support feedback cycles, but Small Improvements covers a broader continuous performance workflow.

Where we differ: Small Improvements is lightweight SaaS. Blik is lightweight and open source. Same idea about simplicity, different control model.

Self-hosting is a reason to consider Blik; integrated 1-on-1s and objectives are reasons to consider Small Improvements.

What Blik Does

  • 360° multi-rater feedback
  • Token-based reviewer access
  • Role-specific questionnaires
  • Automated people analytics
  • Performance insights

What Blik doesn't do: OKRs, 1-on-1s, continuous feedback, or engagement surveys. There is no SSO/SAML, no native HRIS connectors, and no native mobile app; custom integrations use the REST API and webhooks.

What Small Improvements Does

  • 360° feedback cycles
  • 1-on-1 meeting notes
  • Objectives & key results
  • Continuous feedback
  • Praise & recognition

Broader scope: Covers more of the continuous performance management workflow.

Blik uses category-aggregated reports, but confidentiality depends on invitation method, permissions, reporting thresholds, and response content. Reviewer emails can be retained on invitation tokens linked to responses; anonymity is not guaranteed against administrators.

Feature Comparison

360° Feedback Cycles

Blik:

Four pre-built questionnaires, token-based reviewer access, category-aggregated reports, and Dreyfus skill-level mapping.

Small Improvements:

Flexible feedback cycles with custom questions and anonymous feedback options. Confirm visibility settings for your workflow.

Continuous Feedback

Blik:

Not included. Focused on structured 360° review cycles only.

Small Improvements:

Built-in praise, feedback requests, and continuous feedback between cycles. Core feature.

1-on-1 Meetings

Blik:

Not included. Use your preferred tool (Notion, Google Docs, etc.) for meeting notes.

Small Improvements:

Shared meeting notes, talking points, action items. Integrated with feedback and objectives.

Analytics & Insights

Blik:

Automated people analytics: Dreyfus competency model, perception-gap analysis, peer benchmarks, and trends against previous cycles.

Small Improvements:

Reports on feedback completion, ratings trends, and objectives progress. Compare the available reports against your team's requirements.

Which Should You Choose?

Choose Blik If...

  • You want self-hosting capability and data ownership
  • You prefer open source over proprietary software
  • You need flat-rate or free pricing (no per-user costs)
  • 360° feedback is your primary need
  • You want automated people analytics (Dreyfus model)
  • You want category-aggregated reports
  • You're comfortable without 1-on-1s/OKRs in same tool
Try Blik

Choose Small Improvements If...

  • You want an all-in-one lightweight performance tool
  • 1-on-1s and continuous feedback are important
  • You need objectives/goals tracking integrated
  • You prefer fully-managed SaaS (no self-hosting)
  • You're okay with per-user pricing
  • You want praise and recognition alongside review cycles
  • You want a more comprehensive feature set
Visit Small Improvements

Both Are Great—Choose Your Priority

If you need a lightweight, integrated performance management workflow and don't mind SaaS, Small Improvements is excellent.

If you want focused 360° feedback with data ownership, open source transparency, and no per-user costs, Blik is your choice.
