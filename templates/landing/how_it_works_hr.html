    Or upload your org chart

    Upload a CSV with name, email, department and manager. Blik builds the org chart and knows who reviews whom.

    • Blik recognises the column names in English, German and Dutch. You check the mapping on a preview of the first rows.
    • Each manager gets a unit with the people who report to them. Units stack.
    • Or build the chart by hand: create units, drag people between them, drag a unit under another.
    • Import again and Blik replaces the chart. People who left keep their history and only leave their unit.
    {% elif step.slug == 'round' %}

    Or start a round for everyone from the org chart

    Pick a questionnaire, tick the feedback categories, choose "from the org chart". Blik does the rest.

    • One cycle per person, with the reviewers already filled in.
    • Blik skips people the chart gives no reviewers, and tells you who.
    • You see a preview before anything is sent.
    • Everyone who has to fill in forms gets one email with every link they need, their own self-assessment included. A person with a single form gets a normal invitation.
    • The email also carries a personal overview page: the forms "about you" and "about others", with progress. The link works for 30 days. After that, the page sends a fresh link on request.
    {% elif step.slug == 'report' %}

    The report as a PDF

    • When the round completes, Blik finalises the report within five minutes and emails it. You choose whether the PDF goes along.
    • Cover page, executive summary, a radar per reviewer group, section scores, and question details with the self-versus-others gap.
    • Admins download or regenerate the PDF from the report page.
    • We keep PDFs for 30 days by default and then delete them. The report data stays.
