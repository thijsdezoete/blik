Why Choose Blik

Token-Based Anonymous Feedback

Reviewers use token-based links, and reports aggregate feedback by category with configurable thresholds. Responses retain a token reference, which may link to an invitation email. Confidentiality depends on invitation method, permissions, thresholds, and feedback content—not a guarantee against database administrators identifying reviewers.

Self-Hosted Data Control

Deploy on your own infrastructure with complete data sovereignty. No third-party SaaS provider stores or accesses your team's performance feedback. Host on-premise, private cloud, or any server you control.

AGPL Open Source License

Free to use, modify, and deploy under AGPL-3.0 license. Zero licensing costs for any team size. If you host a modified public version, share improvements back to the community. No proprietary vendor lock-in.

Docker Compose Deployment

Production-ready deployment with single docker compose up command. Includes web-based setup wizard for initial configuration. No DevOps expertise required—runs on any Docker-compatible infrastructure.

4 Pre-Built Research-Backed Questionnaires

Includes Professional Skills 360 Review, Software Engineering 360 Review, Manager 360 Review, and 360 Degree Feedback. Customize these templates or use them as supplied.

Full REST API & Webhook Support

REST API with Bearer token authentication and four HMAC-signed webhook events. Generous limits: 1,000 requests/hour per token; 100/hour for anonymous requests.

Tools to Support GDPR Obligations

Self-hosting offers infrastructure control, and managed hosting runs in the EU. Export and deletion tools support GDPR obligations; your organization remains responsible for lawful processing, configuration, access, retention, and its compliance processes.

Open Data Standards

PostgreSQL database with Django ORM. Standard JSON exports for all data. Migrate to other systems without vendor restrictions or export fees. Full database access for custom queries and reporting.

Mobile-Responsive Interface

Professional web interface works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. No app store installation required. Modern web standards with accessibility support (WCAG 2.1 guidelines).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 360-degree feedback?

A 360-degree feedback session is where an employee gets feedback from peers, managers, and if applicable, their direct reports. This gives you a better perspective on their performance and areas to develop. When you pair this with a self-assessment, it shows you where there might be gaps. Maybe you're being too hard on yourself in some areas, or too confident in others. Those mismatches are where the real growth opportunities are.

How does Blik ensure reviewer anonymity?

Reviewers use token-based links, and reports aggregate feedback by category with configurable thresholds. Responses retain a token reference, which may link to an invitation email. Confidentiality depends on invitation method, permissions, thresholds, and feedback content; it is not guaranteed against administrators with database access.

What are the system requirements for self-hosting?

Runs comfortably on a 1 GB VPS for typical team sizes (512 MB minimum). Blik runs on systems supporting Docker; the Docker Compose setup includes a PostgreSQL database.

What questionnaires does Blik include, and how do I choose the right one for my team?

There are currently 4 default questionnaires to choose from; ranging from relatively quick & easy to very specific, detailed and advanced. You can also create your own questionnaires, but these 4 should get you started without having to think of all these questions up front.

Can I customize the questionnaires?

Yes! The application comes with 4 default questionnaires for different roles and needs, and you can create your own with no restrictions. We have multiple types of answer modes like Likert scales, multiple choice, and free text. The questionnaires can contain multiple segments which work well on mobile devices too.

Are my custom questionnaires and questions kept private?

Yes. Questionnaires and questions you add are yours and stay yours. On the hosted service they live in your organization's own account and are never shared with, visible to, or reused for other customers. Nobody on our side reads your questionnaire content either, unless you explicitly ask us to, for example to help with a support request. Self-hosted installs never send anything to us at all. See the privacy policy and terms.

Is Blik GDPR compliant?

EU hosting and self-hosting do not automatically establish GDPR compliance. Blik provides export and deletion tools to support your obligations. Your organization remains responsible for lawful processing, configuration, access controls, retention, and compliance processes.

How is Blik different from Lattice, Culture Amp, and 15Five?

Blik is AGPL-3.0 software you can self-host free, with a one-time hosted review round for €{{ plan_single_price }} or managed EU hosting subscriptions from €{{ plan_saas_price }}/month for up to {{ plan_saas_max }} employees. Lattice, Culture Amp, and 15Five offer broader hosted performance-management suites with different modules and pricing. Compare features, deployment, and dated vendor prices.

What email providers are supported?

Blik supports almost all email providers. We allow you to use your own credentials or even your own Gmail address.

Can reviewers save progress and return later?

Yes, it's actually implicit. Your progress is saved on every step. Reviewers each have their own unique URL, and this allows them to come back later and continue where they left off safely and easily.

What happens if there are too few responses?

We try to keep anonymity in mind here, so we have a default (changeable) threshold of required answers in order to not be able to take any one response from someone directly. For super small teams you can just set this value to 1 and you will see all answers always. This setting also doesn't apply to managers, who I believe usually provide feedback which you can (should?) act upon.

How are reports generated and shared?

A report gets triggered either by all invited members finalizing the questionnaire, or by an application admin who closes the report manually. The report itself shows an executive overview plus a detailed in-depth view of all the questions and the aggregated answers per group (self, peers, managers). A report can be generated at will even if some reviewers haven't finished answering all their questions yet. It takes whatever they've filled out so far and leaves the rest of the questions blank.

Can I migrate from another 360 tool to Blik?

Probably yes, but we don't have a good import and haven't tested this. We didn't end up using any other software, so if you can provide us with samples from other tools, we can try to make this work.

Is there a mobile app?

No, you can't find Blik in the app stores, but our web based application works across all modern mobile devices. Benefit is that this also doesn't require installation or setups on user devices.

What license is Blik released under?

AGPL-3.0 (GNU Affero General Public License v3.0). This license grants unlimited rights to use, modify, and deploy Blik commercially or privately. Key requirement: if you host a modified version accessible over a network, you must make your source code changes available. This ensures community improvements (like LDAP integration or SSO support) benefit all users. For private internal deployments, no code sharing is required.

How do I get support for self-hosted installations?

The repo's issues and discussion tabs are where the support will most likely be given and found. I try to keep things documented that are important too. Enterprise customers will receive priority updates and a more direct line if they prefer.

Can I white-label Blik for clients?

Yes, you can. The AGPL license allows you to white label this if you want to. Most things are pretty easy to modify or built in as variables already.

What databases are supported?

Any Django-compatible database is supported. I recommend using Postgres 15 or higher. The built in standalone Dockerfile uses SQLite to make things easier for anyone to get started from the get go.

How often is Blik updated?

This depends on my time and paid users as well as feedback from the community. I hope there will be many community updates, which eventually will lead to other maintainers as well. If you self-host you should probably check in every once in a while. There is no mechanism for this yet. For hosted customers, updates go live immediately after a few minutes when I push them.

Can I run multiple review cycles simultaneously?

Yes. Cycles are lightly implemented, meaning there is no enforcing of every reviewee having to be on the same cycle. There are no limits on active cycles and no problem having multiple people in different cycles simultaneously.

What analytics and insights does Blik provide?

The report after the questionnaire is finished contains an executive overview that highlights gaps between how you see yourself and how others see you. It spots things like impostor syndrome or undervaluation of your own contributions. You'll see key strengths and weaknesses, plus a bar chart showing self vs others comparison. It also contains a radar chart with the different layers (self, peers, managers) averaged responses. The insights differ per person and questionnaire of course, but in general it should provide actionable insights.

Is there an API for integrations?

Blik includes a REST API with Bearer token authentication and four HMAC-signed webhook events. Generous limits: 1,000 requests/hour per token; 100/hour for anonymous requests. Open interactive API documentation.

How do I invite reviewers?

You can share a link that generates reviewer tokens or send email invitations. Email invitations can associate a token with an email address, and responses retain their token reference. Shared links do not support email reminders. Explain your invitation method and confidentiality limits to reviewers.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You get a full account and organization JSON export. This makes it easy to back up your data or migrate to another system if you need to.
