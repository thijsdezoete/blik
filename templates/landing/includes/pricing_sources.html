Sources & pricing assumptions

Pricing verified October 2026 for published vendor rates. Culture Amp requires a quote. Prices may change; confirm your package with the vendor.

  1. Lattice pricing: Performance $10, Goals & OKRs $8, Engagement $4, Grow $4, and Compensation $6 per seat/month. Performance alone to all five modules totals $10–32 per seat/month, or $6,000–19,200/year for 50 seats. All contracts are billed annually, with a $4,000 annual minimum. The upper figure is the sum of listed modules, not a quoted bundle.
  2. 15Five pricing: Engage $4, Perform $11, and Total Platform $16 per user/month, billed annually. At 50 seats that is $2,400, $6,600, or $9,600/year respectively. Perform and Total Platform include 360° feedback; Engage is an engagement-survey plan.
  3. Culture Amp plans: no public prices; annual billing and a sales quote are required. User-reported G2 figures of $9–14/user/month and $4,500+ annual minimums are not published vendor prices and were not independently reverified.
  4. Blik source and deployment guide: AGPL-3.0; self-host free on a small EU VPS (≈€10-50/month). Get it running in under 30 minutes with Docker Compose, allowing additional time for configuration and backups.

Blik prices are in EUR; vendor prices above are in USD. These comparisons do not subtract across currencies. Plan scope, minimums, taxes, and optional services affect the final quote.
