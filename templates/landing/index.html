How it works

Add people, pick a questionnaire

Add the person being reviewed, choose one of four questionnaires or your own, and list who should give feedback.

Reviewers answer through a link

Each reviewer gets a private link by email. No account, no app, works on a phone. Reminders go out automatically.

Read the report

Scores per category, self versus others, and a skill and initiative level with concrete next steps. Share it with the person or keep it for the conversation.

Reports

Reports people actually read

One page per person, with the numbers that matter and the gaps that start a useful conversation.

  • Scores per competency, from every reviewer group
  • Self-assessment next to what others said, with the biggest gaps called out
  • A skill level on the Dreyfus model and an initiative level on the five levels of agency
  • Strengths, development areas and written comments
Questionnaires

A questionnaire for every role

Four research-backed questionnaires come with Blik. Use them as they are, edit them, or write your own.

  • Professional Skills 360, for any role
  • Software Engineering 360, for engineers
  • Manager 360, for people leads
  • 360 Degree Feedback, twelve questions for a quick round

Likert scales, multiple choice and free text. Your own questionnaires stay private to your organisation.

The framework

A shared language for skill

Every report places the person on the five stages of the Dreyfus model of skill acquisition, from Novice to Expert, and on the five levels of agency. Growth becomes a concrete next stage instead of a vague rating.

  • Skill level, derived from the competency scores
  • Initiative level, derived from the agency questions
  • One performance matrix quadrant that combines the two
  • Concrete next steps for the stage the person is at

How anonymity works

Reviewers answer through token links, never accounts, and reports aggregate by category with a minimum response threshold. A database administrator could in principle link a token to an invitation; nothing in the interface does.

Questions

Can I pay for one review round without a subscription?

Yes. The Single Round is €{{ plan_single_price }} once for a pack of {{ plan_single_max }} review cycles: a hosted feedback round for {{ plan_single_max }} employees. More people? Buy more packs: {{ plan_single_example }} employees = {{ plan_single_example_packs }} packs = €{{ plan_single_example_total }}. No subscription, and no charge for reviewers. See what's included.

How does Blik ensure reviewer anonymity?

Reviewers use token-based links, and reports aggregate feedback by category with configurable thresholds. Responses retain a token reference, which may link to an invitation email. Confidentiality depends on invitation method, permissions, thresholds, and feedback content; it is not guaranteed against administrators with database access.

Is Blik GDPR compliant?

EU hosting and self-hosting do not automatically establish GDPR compliance. Blik provides export and deletion tools to support your obligations. Your organization remains responsible for lawful processing, configuration, access controls, retention, and compliance processes.

Can I customize the questionnaires?

Yes! The application comes with 4 default questionnaires for different roles and needs, and you can create your own with no restrictions. We have multiple types of answer modes like Likert scales, multiple choice, and free text. The questionnaires can contain multiple segments which work well on mobile devices too.

Can I export my data?

Yes. You get a full account and organization JSON export. This makes it easy to back up your data or migrate to another system if you need to.
