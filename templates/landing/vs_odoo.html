Focused vs All-in-One

The two products solve different problems. Odoo is an integrated business suite; Blik is a single-purpose 360° feedback tool. The right choice depends on whether you need the wider platform.

Blik: Focused 360° Feedback

  • Single purpose: 360° multi-rater feedback
  • Four questionnaires: software engineering, manager, professional, quick
  • Feedback access: token-based invitations and category-aggregated reports
  • Automated analytics: Dreyfus skill levels, perception gaps
  • Lightweight to run: typical use fits a small VPS

What Blik doesn't cover: no ERP modules, no SSO/SAML, no native HRIS connectors, and no native mobile app (it uses responsive web design).

Odoo: Integrated Business Suite

  • Full ERP: sales, inventory, accounting, manufacturing, HR
  • HR & appraisals: available within the wider suite
  • Modular: enable only the apps you need
  • Open-source Community plus paid Enterprise edition
  • One vendor: a single system for many business processes

If your business needs an integrated ERP with HR included, Odoo is worth evaluating for its connected apps and shared business workflows.

The Scope Trade-off

If you need a full ERP — sales, inventory, accounting, HR, manufacturing — Odoo makes sense as an integrated ecosystem. If you just need performance reviews, Blik covers that in a focused tool without the wider platform, with a REST API and webhooks for custom integrations. Both are legitimate depending on the scope of what you're building.

What to Consider

  • Setup scope: Configuring modules is part of the initial work
  • Learning: A broad suite has more to learn than a single-purpose tool
  • Upgrades: Custom modules should be tested on major version changes
  • Support: Vendor and partner plans are available
  • Resources: A full ERP typically needs a larger deployment
  • Integration: Deeply integrating external systems can require development
