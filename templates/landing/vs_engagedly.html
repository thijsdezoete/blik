Focused Reviews or a Broader Workflow

Blik

Organize review cycles and interpret feedback using trends, benchmarks, and perception-gap insights.

Blik has no SSO/SAML, native HRIS connectors, or native mobile app. REST API and webhooks support custom integration; the interface is responsive web.

Engagedly

Combine performance reviews with goals, learning, engagement, and recognition workflows. This broader scope can suit teams seeking one platform for several people processes.

Evaluate the modules, administration roles, and reporting options your team will actually use in a vendor demo.

Blik uses token-based reviewer access and category-aggregated reports. Invitation method, permissions, reporting thresholds, and response content affect confidentiality. Invitations can retain reviewer email addresses linked through tokens; this is not an administrator-proof anonymity guarantee.

Recognition and Feedback

Engagedly includes recognition and gamification alongside its performance tools. These may suit teams that want participation and recognition workflows in the same platform.

Blik does not include badges or leaderboards. It concentrates on structured review cycles and reports. Choose based on the workflow you need rather than assuming one approach produces better outcomes for every team.

The Honest Take

Engagedly suits teams seeking performance, learning, and engagement workflows together. Blik suits teams seeking a dedicated, open-source 360° feedback tool with a choice of self-hosting or EU hosting.
