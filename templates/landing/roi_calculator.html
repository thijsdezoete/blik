How We Calculate These Numbers

Sources checked October 2026. We use a fixed illustrative conversion of USD 1 ≈ EUR 0.92, not a live exchange-rate quote. Culture Amp inputs are unverified user-reported assumptions.

Lattice Pricing

Lattice's published rates range from $10/seat/month for Performance to $32 for the sum of all five listed modules, billed annually with a $4,000 annual minimum (October 2026).

We use the $21 midpoint × 0.92 × 12 = €231.84/user/year, with the minimum converted to €3,680/year. The midpoint is an illustrative module mix, not a published bundle price.

Culture Amp Pricing

Culture Amp requires a quote and does not publish prices. The previously cited $9–14/user/month and $4,500 annual minimum are user-reported (G2), not published; not independently reverified in October 2026.

For illustration only, we use approximately €126/user/year from the $11.50 midpoint, with an approximate €4,100 annual minimum. These are rounded planning assumptions, not exact currency conversions. Obtain a vendor quote before using this estimate for a decision.

15Five Pricing

We use approximately €120/user/year after conversion. We apply no minimum because none is published on that pricing page; confirm contract terms with the vendor.
