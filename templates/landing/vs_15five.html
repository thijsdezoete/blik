Feedback Survey Design

Blik's Flexible Approach

  • Custom questionnaires: Create new surveys for each cycle
  • 6 question types: Rating, Likert, text, single/multiple choice, scale
  • Weighted scoring: Prioritize what matters
  • No forced repetition: Change questions cycle-to-cycle
  • Psychological insights: Dreyfus mapping and perception gaps
  • Focus on 360°: Structured, deep reviews

Keep questions fresh by designing each review cycle to fit.

15Five's Library Approach

  • Question library: Choose from preselected questions
  • Time-triggered check-ins: Scheduled on a cadence
  • Continuous feedback: Weekly check-ins plus reviews
  • Engagement surveys: Built-in survey programs
  • OKR tracking: Objectives tied to check-ins

Consistent cadence keeps a steady feedback rhythm for the team.

Two Valid Designs

Continuous feedback and scheduled 360° reviews serve different needs. 15Five's time-triggered check-ins suit teams that want a steady weekly rhythm and OKR-aligned tracking. Blik's custom questionnaires suit teams that run structured review cycles and want to change questions each cycle. Neither is inherently better — the right choice depends on whether your culture prefers frequent lightweight check-ins or periodic in-depth reviews.
