Review Confidentiality

Blik's reporting safeguards

Reviewers use tokenized links and reports aggregate feedback by category. Responses link to tokens that may contain invitation emails. This is not an anonymity guarantee against database administrators.

Invitation methods, access permissions, thresholds, and identifying comments all affect confidentiality.

Evaluate Culture Amp's settings

Review the visibility rules, report thresholds, retention terms, and administrator access for your chosen workflow. Explain these to participants before collecting feedback.

Which Platform Is Right for You?

Choose Blik If You:

  • Want token-based review access and aggregated reports
  • Want flat, transparent pricing
  • Prefer self-hosted data control
  • Value open source verification
  • Want focused 360° feedback, not a broader engagement suite
