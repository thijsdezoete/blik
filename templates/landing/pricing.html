Pricing Questions

Why is self-hosting free?

Blik is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license. Self-hosting has no software license fee. Hosted plans cover managed EU infrastructure, updates, backups, and support.

What's the catch with €{{ plan_saas_price }}/month?

No catch. It's a flat €{{ plan_saas_price }}/month for up to {{ plan_saas_max }} employees, all features included. I wanted pricing that doesn't require a spreadsheet to calculate. If you grow past {{ plan_saas_max }} employees, you move to the €{{ plan_ent_price }}/month tier for up to {{ plan_ent_max }} employees. That's it.

Are there any hidden costs?

None. €{{ plan_saas_price }} is €{{ plan_saas_price }}. No setup fees, no per-user charges, no feature restrictions, no surprise renewal increases. The only way your bill goes up is if you grow past {{ plan_saas_max }} employees and need the larger plan.

What happens if I go over {{ plan_saas_max }} employees on the €{{ plan_saas_price }} plan?

The plan covers up to {{ plan_saas_max }} active employees receiving reviews; team members are not capped. Contact us about the €{{ plan_ent_price }}/month Enterprise plan for up to {{ plan_ent_max }} employees, or custom pricing for larger teams. In-app plan changes are not available.

Can I switch from self-hosted to hosted later?

Yes, but we don't have an automated migration tool yet. You'd need to export your data from your self-hosted instance and we can help you import it into a hosted account. If this is something you need, reach out and we'll figure it out.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits or education?

Not formally yet, but reach out. For non-profits and educational institutions, I'm happy to work something out. Self-hosting is always free regardless.

What payment methods do you accept?

Credit card via Stripe for hosted plans. We handle EUR, USD, and GBP. Invoicing is available for annual contracts on the Enterprise plan.

Is there a refund policy?

{{ trial_days }}-day free trial means you can try before you pay. After that, if you cancel, you're billed for the current month and that's it. No refunds on partial months, but you keep access until the end of your billing period.

How does pricing compare to building this in-house?

Self-hosting lets you use the existing open-source application rather than build your own. Get it running in under 30 minutes with Docker Compose, then allow time for configuration, backups, and ongoing maintenance.
