Questions About the Single Round

What do I get for €{{ plan_single_price }}?

One purchase gives you {{ plan_single_max }} review cycles. A review cycle is one employee reviewed with one questionnaire: invitations, anonymous responses, and an individual report. That covers a feedback round for up to {{ plan_single_max }} employees.

Do the review cycles expire?

No. Unused review cycles stay on your account until you use them. Deleting a review cycle does not give it back.

What happens when the round is finished?

Nothing is taken away. Review cycles that are still running finish normally, and every report stays available in your account. To start new review cycles after you have used all {{ plan_single_max }}, buy another round.

Can I buy another round later?

Yes. Each purchase adds {{ plan_single_max }} review cycles to the same account, and you can buy the next round from Settings inside your account. There is no subscription and nothing renews on its own.

Do reviewers pay or need an account?

No. Reviewers are free and unlimited. They open a personal link, answer the questionnaire, and are done.

Can I switch to a subscription later?

Yes. A subscription is started from Settings inside your account, and your unused review cycles stay on the account. Subscriptions start with a {{ trial_days }}-day free trial — credit card required; cancel before it ends and you won't be charged.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes. Payment is by card through Stripe, and Stripe emails you an invoice for the purchase.

Can I get a refund?

Unused rounds are refunded on request within 14 days of purchase. See the terms for how to reach us.

What if I have more than {{ plan_single_max }} employees?

Buy more than one round, or choose the EU Hosted subscription: €{{ plan_saas_price }}/month for up to {{ plan_saas_max }} employees with unlimited review cycles. Compare the plans.
