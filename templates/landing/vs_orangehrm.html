Deployment and Operations

Both products offer open-source self-hosting. Blik uses Django and PostgreSQL with Docker Compose; OrangeHRM uses a PHP-based stack. Choose an option your team can operate and maintain.

Self-hosting either product means planning updates, access control, backups, and recovery testing. An open-source license alone does not guarantee availability or prevent data loss.

Managed hosting is an alternative when your team prefers the provider to operate the service. Compare the support and service terms of the specific plan.

Compare the Workflow You Need

Blik concentrates on structured review cycles. OrangeHRM combines HR administration with performance-management workflows. Try representative tasks with reviewers, managers, and administrators before choosing.

Blik has no SSO/SAML, native HRIS connectors, or native mobile app. It offers responsive web access and a REST API plus webhooks for custom integrations, not a replacement for leave, time, or recruitment tools.

Feedback Confidentiality

Blik uses token-based access and category-aggregated reports. Invitation method, permissions, reporting thresholds, and response content affect confidentiality. Reviewer emails may be stored with invitation tokens linked to responses, so anonymity is not guaranteed against administrators.

For OrangeHRM, confirm who can see reviewer identities and individual responses, which aggregation controls apply, and how exports behave in your selected edition. Evaluate both products against your organization's confidentiality requirements.
